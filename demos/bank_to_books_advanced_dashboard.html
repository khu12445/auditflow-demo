Reusable Workflow

  1. Upload client's bank statement.
  2. Enter owner/client and business nature.
  3. Review extracted names and categories.
  4. Review suggested journal entries.
  5. Use Ledger → Trial Balance → P&L → Balance Sheet → Cash Flow.
  6. For final accounts, add opening balances, inventory, receivables, payables, fixed assets, loans, owner capital/drawings and non-bank transactions.
