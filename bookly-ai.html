What the project explores

One workspace for the accounting journey.

Bookly.AI brings together core bookkeeping processes in a connected, review-oriented design. Some parts are still being refined as part of a local application project.

▤

Bank statement processing

Organize imported bank activity and prepare consistent transaction records from source files.

⇄

Suggested classification

Explore assisted account categorization with visible exceptions rather than automatic final posting.

✎

Draft journal entries

Move transactions toward debit-and-credit entries that can be checked against supporting records.

✓

Human review controls

Review, adjust and verify financial records before treating them as final accounting outputs.

◫

Financial reporting views

Connect reviewed records to ledger, profit-and-loss and balance-sheet reporting concepts.

◇

Connected bookkeeping workspace

Bring together customer, vendor, invoice and reconciliation work in a more structured environment.

Designed workflow

From source documents to reliable decisions.

A practical accounting process should be explainable at every step — especially when AI is involved.

01Import

Start with bank transactions or accounting documents.

02Structure

Read fields, standardize records and flag gaps.

03Suggest

Prepare candidate categories and journal entries.

04Review

Have an accountant verify purpose, evidence and accuracy.

05Report

Prepare provisional ledger and reporting views.

Responsible accounting automation

Automation supports the accountant. It doesn't replace the controls.

Design principles

✓Keep source data and suggested postings traceable.
✓Require review for uncertain transaction classifications.
✓Do not treat bank activity alone as complete accrual books.
✓Protect confidential financial records and use synthetic data in public examples.

Project status

Bookly.AI is a personal accounting software project, developed and iterated as a local application. The emphasis is on bank-to-books workflows, assisted bookkeeping and controlled reporting.

Development disclosure: This website is a public case study, not a login page or production accounting service. Features described are product goals or explored workflows; complete unattended AI bookkeeping has not been independently verified.
